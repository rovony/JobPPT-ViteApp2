## DECK-WIDE OBSERVATIONS

### Cross-slide consistency wins

- Eyebrow / headline / subhead / footer rhythm holds across slots 5 → 12 (CS1 act). Cream eyebrow → bold display headline with italic coral accent → muted italic subhead → hairline-bordered viz cards → mono kicker + source line.
- Coral case color is consistently reserved for CS1 throughout the act — no off-token hex codes encountered in any of the 12 slides.
- Hairline panels (`1px solid var(--cream-hairline)`) are used systematically as the data-container chrome from slot 6 onward, matching v2.1 contract.
- The shared-element morph (`layoutId` lung from slot 5 → slot 6) lands cleanly with no flicker.
- Page meta `NN / 35` in the chrome of every slot (now uniform after slot-10 fix).

### Cross-slide consistency concerns flagged for follow-up

- **Architectural overflow at 1366×768** on slots 7 and 8: the `SlideFrame` viz cell + footer cannot simultaneously hold a tall column-stack and a closing ribbon at the narrow viewport. This is a deck-wide pattern issue (not isolated to these two slides) and may recur on slots 17 / 18 / 25 / 26 — all of which use a similar three-column + closing-ribbon composition. **Recommendation**: a structural pass on `SlideGrid` to add an optional `closing-ribbon` row between viz and footer, OR a `useViewport()` hook that hides the closing ribbon below 1500 px and surfaces it as the subhead instead.
- **Slot 10 architectural drift**: it does not use `SlideFrame`. Chrome, headline, source line, and page meta are absolute-positioned. Pre-existing — out of scope for this sweep — but flagged so a future migration agent knows to bring slot 10 onto `SlideFrame` for consistency.

### Narrative-flow gaps

- None observed. The CS1 arc reads as a single editorial assertion per slide:
  - 5 (divider) → 6 (background, what we're up against) → 7 (challenge, why it's hard) → 8 (strategy, three decisions) → 9 (build, sequential workflow) → 10 (fit, no bias) → 11 (exposure match, ±3 %) → 12 (exposure-response, flat = safe). The hand-offs are clean and the case beat is preserved at every step.
