# CS1 Reduction Plan

Target: deliver Case 1 in about 10 minutes, with the live story focused on the clinical pharmacology decision rather than the full PAH and regulatory background.

## Reduction Strategy

- Keep the live spine: question -> disease/drug setup -> trial constraint -> PopPK bridge -> PK/PD sanity check -> leadership/workflow -> outcome -> handoff.
- Move encyclopedic context to backup/Q&A: PAH treatment history and the two-precedent EMA/FDA architecture slide.
- Reduce speaker notes to what must be said in the room. Keep caveats in cues/Q&A rather than spoken paragraphs.
- Use 120-130 wpm as the planning rate, with room for pauses.
