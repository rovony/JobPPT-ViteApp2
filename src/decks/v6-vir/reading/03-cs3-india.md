# Case 03 · Ivosidenib India Reliance

This case is the leadership and reliance beat. It shows how a global clinical
pharmacology dossier can answer a local-data question when a pre-approval local
trial is not feasible, while still naming the evidence gaps honestly.

Presenter guardrail:

- Do not imply local Indian PK/PD data existed before approval.
- Do not present Rule 101 as automatic approval.
- Keep the argument as convergence: somatic mechanism, global PopPK,
  exposure-response, intrinsic and extrinsic factors, global safety experience,
  and a Phase 4 commitment for residual uncertainty.

Key phrases:

- "The convergence was the case."
- "The gap did not disappear; it was named and bounded."
- "Quantitative pharmacology answered whether the global evidence could
  extrapolate; partner functions made the pathway and execution hold."
