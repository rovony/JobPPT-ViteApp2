# Case 03 · AI / Pharazi

This case is a personal research and architecture example. It should be presented as clinical pharmacology workflow judgment, not a product pitch, sponsor deployment, or regulatory acceptance claim.

The core idea is traceability. As clinical pharmacology workflows become more computational, every number, assumption, and output needs an inspectable chain from input to decision record. Deterministic tools, human review, privacy boundaries, and audit trails matter more than agent novelty.

Key concepts:

- ICH M15-aligned documentation pressure
- Deterministic analysis components
- Human-in-the-loop review
- Versioned assumptions and output traceability
- AI systems that preserve expert accountability
