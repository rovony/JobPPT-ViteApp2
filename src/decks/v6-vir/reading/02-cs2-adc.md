# Case 02 · ADC Multi-Analyte PK

This case is framed as a transferable clinical pharmacology leadership credential. It does not claim confidential sponsor-specific ADC data, nor that ADCs represent most of the speaker's career.

The scientific point is that ADC dose strategy depends on an analyte map: conjugated antibody, total antibody, released payload, and derived exposure metrics can each support different clinical interpretations. A defensible dose rationale starts by naming the decision and matching the exposure metric to that decision.

Key concepts:

- Multi-analyte PK strategy
- Analyte-to-biology mapping
- Population PK model architecture
- TMDD-like nonlinearity as a decision point
- Exposure-response metric selection
- Cross-functional dose-rationale leadership
