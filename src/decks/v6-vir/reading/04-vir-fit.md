# Vir Landing

The Vir section translates the three cases into an interview fit argument. It should not imply prior Vir program ownership or a direct PRO-XTEN track record.

The intended message is that Vir's clinical pharmacology environment benefits from model discipline under evidence constraints: viral biology, immune pharmacology, dose timing, and high-consequence decisions need clear assumptions and portable evidence.

Leadership emphasis:

- Learn the portfolio before prescribing solutions
- Map evidence gaps to decisions
- Build cross-functional decision cadence
- Preserve interpretability as analysis speed increases
- Establish reusable standards for simulations, assumptions, and decision records
