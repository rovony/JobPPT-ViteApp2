# Case 02 · Asparlas Efficient Design

This case is the efficient-design bridge between pediatric extrapolation and
regional reliance. The problem was not whether calaspargase pegol had a
scientific basis; the problem was whether an adult Ph-negative ALL program
could be made decision-grade without an endpoint-powered adult trial.

Presenter guardrail:

- Say this was imported from the prior Asparlas source case and re-keyed as
  Case 02 for the seminar story.
- Keep the claim to the public decision arc: FDA Type A engagement, N=60,
  simulated primary, optimal design, and a 36% adult trial-size reduction.
- Do not turn this into a general claim that small trials are automatically
  acceptable. The point is precision, pre-specified assumptions, and agency
  review before execution.

Key phrases:

- "Model precision is not endpoint power."
- "Stacked methods, but still reviewable."
- "Efficient design only works when the uncertainty is explicit."
